What this is
Coffee, once a week, with other people.
What this isn't
Networking, a funnel, a "community" with a content calendar.
What you'll get
Conversation, maybe a friend, definitely caffeine.
What you won't get
A follow-up email.

Coffee and Cowork is a weekly, unstructured gathering of people who occasionally leave their houses. There is no agenda, no keynote, and nobody will ask what you're passionate about. This is not networking — nobody here is building a personal brand or growing a pipeline. We're a few people who like coffee and like talking to each other more than we like talking to our phones.

Bring a laptop if you want to look busy, or don't, and just talk to someone for an hour. If you're hoping to pitch something, you'll have a bad time — everyone else came here to talk to people, not get sold to.
